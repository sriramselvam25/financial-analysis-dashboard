# Interview Walkthrough

**Problem:** executives need a compact view connecting P&L performance, budget control and forecast quality.

**Approach:** I modelled monthly business-unit financial observations and created measures for revenue, gross/operating profit, margins, budget variance, forecast accuracy and YoY growth.

**Design:** the four-page report moves from executive summary through revenue/margin and cost/budget diagnostics to forecast outlook.

**Value:** the model makes actual, budget and forecast comparisons explicit and supports drill-down without losing executive context.
